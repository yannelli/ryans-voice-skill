# Bio and About Copy

Basis: Composed from SKILL.md rules and quoted snippets; no source document (the subject, Sam Rivera, is fictional)

What to notice:
- The first sentence gives the current role, the company, and the scope in numbers.
- Evidence replaces adjectives: "moved all 1,200 shops to a new database in one weekend, with 11 minutes of downtime".
- The speaker bio uses third person, and the About section uses first person.
- The About section lists the work one item per line after a colon lead-in.
- Each version ends on the latest role, the talk, or the most relevant result.

## Speaker bio (third person)

```text
Sam Rivera is the CTO of [Company], which makes scheduling and invoicing software used by 1,200 independent auto repair shops. He leads a team of 9 engineers and wrote the first version of the booking engine in 2022.

Before [Company], Sam ran an IT services firm for 6 years, supporting networks and phone systems for 40 offices and 3 school districts.

His talk covers how [Company] moved all 1,200 shops to a new database in one weekend, with 11 minutes of downtime.
```
