# Cold Email: Open Source Maintainer

Basis: Composed from SKILL.md rules and quoted snippets; no source document

What to notice:
- The compliment is specific and checkable: "your 2.4 release fixed our queue retry bug".
- Two short clauses join with a comma in line one: "I'm the CTO at [Company], we run [library] in production".
- The ask is one question, and the budget line gives dollars and an estimated hour range.
- First name only as the sign-off.

## Open source maintainer

```text
Subject: Paid work on batch export for [library]

Hi Jamie,

I'm the CTO at [Company], we run [library] in production and your 2.4 release fixed our queue retry bug.

Would you take a paid contract to add batch export? The code would go upstream under your license.

The budget is $4,000 for an estimated 30 to 40 hours. I can send the spec today.

Ryan
```
