# Email: Past-Due Notice

Basis: Composed from SKILL.md rules and quoted snippets; no source document

What to notice:
- The subject states the fact and the consequence date: "Invoice 3094 past due, scheduled work pauses 07/15".
- Line one is one sentence with the invoice number, amount, due date, and days late.
- The consequence is flat and cites the agreement: "scheduled work pauses on 07/15 if the invoice is still open."
- A single ask closes the body, written as a condition: "If it's already been mailed, reply with the check number".

## Past-due notice

```text
Subject: Invoice 3094 past due, scheduled work pauses 07/15

Hi Pat,

Invoice 3094 ($640.00, due 06/30/2026) is 10 days past due.

Per the agreement, scheduled work pauses on 07/15 if the invoice is still open. Urgent tickets will still be answered.

The invoice and a payment link are attached (Invoice-3094.pdf). If it's already been mailed, reply with the check number and I'll match it on our end.

Kind regards,

Ryan Yannelli
[Title], [Company]
```
