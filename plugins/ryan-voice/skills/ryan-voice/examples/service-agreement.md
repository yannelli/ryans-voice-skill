# Service Agreement

Basis: Adapted from an undated IT service agreement draft (names, figures, and details changed)

What to notice:
- Numbered caps headings ("1. SERVICES", "5. EXCLUSIONS") give each topic one short block.
- A lead-in ending in a colon introduces each dash list: "The Services covered by this Agreement are:".
- Modifiers and conditions sit in parentheses beside the item: "(1.75x Modifier)", "(or the next business day if Tuesday is a federal holiday)".
- No contractions; fees and consequences are flat sentences with digits.
- Location entries use "Label: Sentence", with the sentence capitalized after the colon.
- The document ends on the signature block.

## Website and point-of-sale support agreement

```text
WEBSITE AND POINT-OF-SALE SUPPORT AGREEMENT (DRAFT)

This Support Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of [Date] between Northside Bakery, LLC, located at [Address] ("Client"), and [Provider], Inc., located at [Address] ("Service Provider").

1. SERVICES

The Services covered by this Agreement are:

- Website Hosting and Updates
- Online Ordering Menu Updates
- Email Administration
- Point-of-Sale Hardware Support
- Receipt/Label Printer Support
- Menu/Signage Design
- After-Hours Support (1.75x Modifier)

2. TERM AND TERMINATION

This Agreement starts on the date above and renews every six (6) months. Either party can end this Agreement with fifteen (15) days' written notice. No fee applies to early termination.

3. PAYMENT

Client will pay a fixed fee of $325 per month, due on the 10th. A $15 late fee is added to any payment received after the 20th.

4. ADDITIONAL LOCATIONS

This Agreement also covers:

a. Farmers Market Stand (Saturdays only): Remote support is included in the monthly fee. Onsite visits are billed at a 1.25x modifier with a 1-hour minimum.
b. Commissary Kitchen: Onsite and remote support are billed at the standard rate in 30-minute increments, with no minimum.

5. EXCLUSIONS

The following are not covered by this Agreement:

- Purchase of hardware or replacement parts
- Third-party software licenses and subscriptions
- Phone/fax lines
- Delivery app accounts (Client manages these directly)
- Work outside the website, email, and point-of-sale scope

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

Client will share login credentials, vendor account details, and similar private data with Service Provider ("Confidential Information").

Service Provider will not share Confidential Information with any third party and will delete it within 30 days after this Agreement ends.

7. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

Service Provider will respond within the following times:

- Critical (point-of-sale or online ordering down): 2-hour maximum response
- Standard (one device or one user affected): next business day
- Website text and photo changes: completed each Tuesday (or the next business day if Tuesday is a federal holiday)

Standard hours are 6 AM to 2 PM, Tuesday through Sunday (the bakery's open hours - work outside them is billed at the After-Hours rate). Critical requests are answered at any hour. A critical request that would exceed the monthly fee requires Client's approval first.

8. SIGNATURES

Signed by the parties as of the date above.

Northside Bakery, LLC

By: ________________________
Name: [Owner Name]
Title: Owner

[Provider], Inc.

By: ________________________
Name: [Name]
Title: President
```
