# Professional Email

Basis: Composed from SKILL.md rules and quoted snippets; no source document

What to notice:
- Subject lines state the ask or the fact, with the date: "Invoice 3094 past due, scheduled work pauses 07/15".
- The first line carries the whole email: what, how much, by when.
- One ask per email, written as a condition: "If you approve by Friday 04/10, ...".
- Attachments are named by file name, and numbers are exact.
- The status update opens with a colon lead-in and puts each finding on its own line.
- Sign-off is "Regards," or "Kind regards," with name and title.

## Approval request

```text
Subject: Laptop quote for the front office, approval needed by 04/10

Hi Morgan,

The quote for 3 replacement laptops is attached (Quote-0418.pdf), $3,387.00 total with setup.

Each laptop is $979.00, and setup with data transfer is $150.00 per machine. The current laptops are 7 years old and no longer get security updates.

If you approve by Friday 04/10, the vendor ships Tuesday and we'll have them set up by 04/17. The vendor's price expires after 04/10.

Regards,

Ryan Yannelli
[Title], [Company]
```
