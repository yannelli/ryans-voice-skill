## LinkedIn post

```text
We hired our first support engineer 6 months ago. Before that, I answered every ticket myself (about 40 a week).

What changed:
- First reply time went from ~5 hours to 22 minutes.
- I got back about 10 hours a week for product work.
- 3 recurring bugs were found because one person read every ticket in order.

The bug fixes alone cut ticket volume by 18% over the next 2 months.
```
