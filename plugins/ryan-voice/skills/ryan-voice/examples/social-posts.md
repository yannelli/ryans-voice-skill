# Social Posts (X and LinkedIn)

Basis: Composed from SKILL.md rules and quoted snippets; no source document

What to notice:
- Each post leads with the number or the claim.
- Digits throughout, including small counts ("2 people", "3 recurring bugs").
- The LinkedIn post introduces its results with a colon lead-in and a dash list.
- Humor is dry and sits inside a fact: "the one that sleeps for 5 seconds on purpose".
- No hashtag stack, no emoji, and no "excited to announce".

## X posts

```text
Cut our test suite from 14 minutes to 3 by running it across 8 processes. The slowest test is now the one that sleeps for 5 seconds on purpose.
```

```text
Support first-reply time was 4.2 hours in March and 11 minutes in August. Same 2 people. We moved the inbox into the app and turned on push notifications.
```

```text

```

## LinkedIn post

```text
We hired our first support engineer 6 months ago. Before that, I answered every ticket myself (about 40 a week).

What changed:
- First reply time went from ~5 hours to 22 minutes.
- I got back about 10 hours a week for product work.
- 3 recurring bugs were found because one person read every ticket in order.

The bug fixes alone cut ticket volume by 18% over the next 2 months.
```
