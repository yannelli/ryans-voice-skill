# Cold Outreach

Basis: Composed from SKILL.md rules and quoted snippets; no source document

What to notice:
- The first line names Ryan's role and the reason for writing to this person.
- Line two is the ask, sized in time or money: "a free 30-minute walk-through", "a paid contract".
- Line three gives the number that backs the ask: "Wi-Fi tickets went from ~25 a month to 3."
- The compliment is specific and checkable: "your 2.4 release fixed our queue retry bug".
- Each message stays under 120 words, with the name as the only sign-off.

## Prospective customer

```text
Subject: Wi-Fi complaints at the Elm Street buildings

Hi Robin,

I run a small IT firm in [Town], and 9 of the last 20 tenant reviews for your Elm Street buildings mention Wi-Fi dropping.

Could I do a free 30-minute walk-through of one building next week?

Last year we fixed the same problem at a 60-unit building on [Street]. Wi-Fi tickets went from ~25 a month to 3.

Ryan Yannelli
[Company] | 555-0142
```

## Open source maintainer

```text
Subject: Paid work on batch export for [library]

Hi Jamie,

I'm the CTO at [Company], we run [library] in production and your 2.4 release fixed our queue retry bug.

Would you take a paid contract to add batch export? The code would go upstream under your license.

The budget is $4,000 for an estimated 30 to 40 hours. I can send the spec today.

Ryan
```
