# Offer Letter: Part-Time Terms Block

Basis: Adapted from a 2021 offer letter (names, figures, and details changed)

What to notice:
- The hours range uses a plain hyphen with the qualifier in parentheses: "16-24 Hours (Flexible)".
- Each label gets one value line, and the schedule condition sits inline: "(set 2 weeks in advance)".
- The benefits line states what is excluded, flat, inside the parentheses.

## Part-time terms block

```text
HOURS & SCHEDULE:
    HOURS PER WEEK:       16-24 Hours (Flexible)
    SCHEDULE:             Weekday afternoons, 1 PM to 6 PM (set 2 weeks in advance)

COMPENSATION:
    BASE PAY:             $21.50 per hour, paid bi-weekly
    BENEFITS:             Paid sick leave as required by state law (health insurance is not offered at this level)
```
