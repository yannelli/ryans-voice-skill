# Before and After: LinkedIn Announcement

Basis: Composed from SKILL.md rules and quoted snippets; no source document

What to notice:
- Announcement phrasing and hashtags are cut, and the post opens on the fact.
- The second sentence gives the mechanism with numbers: "a text within 60 seconds", "15 minutes to claim it".
- It ends on the measured beta result.

```text

```

After:
```text
Waitlists are live for all 1,200 shops. When a slot opens, the next customer gets a text within 60 seconds and has 15 minutes to claim it.

In the 3-week beta, 41% of late cancellations were refilled.
```
