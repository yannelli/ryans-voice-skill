# Terms of Service and Policy

Basis: Composed from SKILL.md rules and quoted snippets; no source document

What to notice:
- Numbered caps sections hold a few one-line commitments each.
- No contractions, and consequences run flat and in order: failed payment, retries, suspension, deletion.
- Exclusions follow a colon lead-in as a dash list.
- Section 5 carries the one dry line, followed by the exclusions.
- A worked case turns the credit rule into dollars: "on the $45 plan, 2 days down in a 30-day month is a $3 credit".

## Terms of service

```text
TERMS OF SERVICE

Last updated: 02/01/2026

1. ACCOUNTS
You must be 18 or older to create an account.
One person or business per account.
You are responsible for all activity under your login.

2. BILLING
Plans are billed monthly on the date you signed up.
If a payment fails, we retry it after 3 days and again after 7 days. If the third attempt fails, the account is suspended and your booking page shows "Temporarily unavailable".
A suspended account is deleted 60 days after suspension. Data cannot be recovered after deletion.

3. CANCELLATION
You can cancel at any time from Settings > Billing. Service continues until the end of the paid month. Partial months are not refunded.

4. ACCEPTABLE USE
The following are not permitted:
- Sending bulk messages to people who did not book with you
- Reselling access to another business
- Splitting large files to get around the 25 MB upload limit (ask us for more storage instead)
- Automated scraping of other accounts' booking pages

5. AVAILABILITY
We plan for the service to be up every hour of every day. The hours it is not are covered below.

The following are excluded:
- Scheduled maintenance, announced by email 48 hours in advance
- Outages at our hosting or payment providers
- Problems caused by your own domain, DNS, or email settings

If the service is down for more than 4 consecutive hours outside these exclusions, you can request a credit for each full day affected. For example, on the $45 plan, 2 days down in a 30-day month is a $3 credit.

6. CHANGES
[Company] will email all account owners 30 days before any change to these terms takes effect.
```

## Data retention policy

```text
DATA RETENTION

[Company] keeps account data while the account is active.

After cancellation:
- Bookings and client records are kept for 90 days, then deleted.
- Invoices are kept for 7 years (to meet tax record requirements).
- Backups that contain the account are overwritten within 35 days of deletion.

To request earlier deletion, email [privacy email] from the account owner's address. Requests are completed within 30 days, and a confirmation is sent when done.
```
