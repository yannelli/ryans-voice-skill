# Texts and DMs

Basis: Composed from SKILL.md rules and quoted snippets; no source document

What to notice:
- No greeting or sign-off in any message.
- Register follows the recipient: lowercase and a mild swear with a friend, full sentences with a contractor.
- Short related clauses join with a comma: "deploy's done, 2 migrations ran clean".
- Numbers stay digits in lowercase texts: "15 min", "41 boxes".
- Deliberate caps carry the one thing that has to land: "Do NOT let him out the front door".
- Dry humor sits inside the fact, with no emoji punchline.
